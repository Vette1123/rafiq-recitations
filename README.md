# Rafiq recitations

Recitation audio served to the [Rafiq](https://vette1123.github.io/rafiq-privacy/) app over GitHub Pages, for recitations no public Qur'an audio host carries in a form the app can play ayah by ayah.

## saud-aljumah/1446/

Sheikh **Saud Al-Jumah** (سعود آل جمعة), his taraweeh prayers of Ramadan 1446 AH, one file per surah (`001.mp3` … `114.mp3`, mono MP3, 64 kbps CBR).

- Source: his official YouTube channel, [القناة الرسمية للقارئ: سعود آل جمعة](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCfjfubzW90LTtTvAvg7hryw), playlist «المصحف المرتل من ليالي رمضان 1446هـ».
- The app plays each surah ayah by ayah. The ayah timings were worked out from the audio by the app's build (`scripts/align-recitation.mjs`); they are not the reciter's.

All rights to these recordings remain with the reciter and his channel. If you hold those rights and want them removed, open an issue and they will be taken down.
